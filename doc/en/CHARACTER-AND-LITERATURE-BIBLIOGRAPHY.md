## 1. Character Design Principles & Visual Perception Psychology

### A. Industry Monographs & Animation Foundations
* **Bancroft, Tom (2006)**. *Creating Characters with Personality: For Film, TV, Animation, Video Games, and Graphic Novels*. Watson-Guptill.  
  *(Personality-driven design, Line of Action, exaggeration, appeal, and expressive hierarchy)*

  *(Shape language, silhouette-first design, gesture sketching, and thumbnail exploration)*

  *(The 12 principles of animation, including Follow-through & Overlapping Action and Appeal)*
* **McCloud, Scott (1993)**. *Understanding Comics: The Invisible Art*. William Morrow Paperbacks.  
  *(Iconic abstraction continuum and viewer identification)*
