# 3D Reconstruction, Rigging, and Stylized Motion References

This reference guide records the academic literature, open-source repositories, professional rigging practices, and motion/pose collections consulted to upgrade Pawzura's multi-snapshot 2D-to-3D pipeline, articulated character rigging, skinning, and procedural/retargeted motion under an **8 GB VRAM** budget.

> **Important boundary:** Items marked **Applied in Pawzura** are implemented in this repository's MIT codebase (`art_runtime/` and `pawzura/`). External models, weights, and datasets listed for comparison retain their own original licenses and are **not** bundled or automatically downloaded unless explicitly documented in [the catalog](../../art_runtime/catalog.json) and [notices](THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md).

## 1. Multi-Snapshot 2D-to-3D and Low-VRAM Staged Architectures

A single front drawing leaves side and back geometry/textures ambiguous, and naive single-view projection blurs facial details when rendered through low-resolution intermediate views. Academic and open-source work converges on **decoupled sequential stages** so heavy models never share VRAM at the same time.

### How Pawzura Uses Staged Splitting on 8 GB VRAM

1. **Stage 1 (Image Generation):** SDXL 1.0 generates a crisp 1024×1024 front character (`runner.py`), then exits.
2. **Stage 2 (Multi-Elevation TripoSR Snapshots + 4-Chart UV Atlas):** TripoSR loads (~2.0 GiB peak), renders **24 or 36 spherical snapshots** across `0°`, `+22°`, and `-16°` elevations at 256×256, queries a **`128³` grid in 8-slice Z chunks**, extracts an oriented triangle mesh, projects all snapshots plus the front and user angle artwork onto vertex colors, and bakes a **`1280×1280` 4-chart (`+X/-X/+Y/-Y`) cylindrical side UV atlas (`bake_texture_atlas`)** with `3×3` outlier despeckling and per-tile EDT gutter dilation before exiting.
3. **Stage 3 (Optional Sequential SDXL Multi-View Texture Polish):** In `sdxl_multiview` mode, an isolated worker loads cached SDXL 1.0 Img2Img with model CPU offload, renders UV-textured views, refines 5 non-front views sequentially (`left_3q → right_3q → facing_left → facing_right → back`) with directional anti-Janus prompts (`strength=0.28`), and re-bakes and blends the UV atlas before exiting.
4. **Stage 4 & 5 (CPU Limb-Detached Rigging & UV 2×-SSAA Baking):** CPU ViTPose + geodesic/heat rigging runs, automatically detaches fused torso-sleeve triangles below the armpit with Z-tiered watertight caps (`_detach_and_cap_limbs`) to unlock safe arm mobility, exits, and the CPU bilinear-UV triangle rasterizer bakes anti-aliased 256 px transparent PNG clips.

## 2. Detailed 3D Character Rigging, Anatomy, and Skinning

### Production Rigging Practices (Blender Rigify / Advanced Skeleton / VRM / Chibi Rigging)

Experienced character riggers prevent volume collapse, joint pinching, and stiff silhouettes by rigging fine anatomical structures rather than a minimal 12-stick skeleton:

- **Pelvis & Multi-Segment Spine (`pelvis -> spine_lower -> chest -> neck -> head`):** Separating pelvis tilt/drop from waist and chest allows natural **spinal counter-rotation** against hip yaw during walking and prevents rubber-hose bending in short chibi torsos.
- **Clavicles (`left_clavicle`, `right_clavicle`):** Anchored near the sternum to lift and protract shoulders naturally when waving or cheering without pinching sleeves into the ribcage.
- **Forearm & Wrist Articulation + Elbow/Knee Pole Vectors:** Separating upper arm, forearm, and hand allows phase-lagged secondary swing, while forward/outward pole vectors keep knees and elbows bending cleanly in 3D without gimbal flips.
- **Reverse Foot Roll (`ankle -> ball -> toe`):** Anchors the ball/metatarsal pivot on the ground plane during heel-off push-off while tilting the ankle upward and keeping toes planted—eliminating "brick-foot" skating.
- **TMJ Jaw Pivot & Facial Controls:** The jaw hinges posteriorly near the ear/TMJ depth (`tmj_pivot`) rather than pulling lips straight down, preserving chin arc and cheek volume alongside curved eyelid closure, brow lift, and pupil gaze offsets.
- **Dynamic Secondary Chains (VRM `VRMSpringBone` / PhysBones):** Hair strands, ears, and coat tails/tails are segmented and driven by damped spring-mass physics rather than rigid head weights.
